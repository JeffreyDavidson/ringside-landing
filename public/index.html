Questions

Who is Ringside built for? +

Ringside is built for wrestling promoters and the teams managing a promotion’s roster, events, matches and championships.

Does Ringside handle tag teams, stables and officials? +

Yes. Tag teams, stables, managers and referees are part of the roster, each with their own status and membership history.

Does Ringside handle ticket sales? +

Ticket sales and payments are outside Ringside’s current feature set. Its focus is the wrestling operation: your people, shows, matches and titles.
